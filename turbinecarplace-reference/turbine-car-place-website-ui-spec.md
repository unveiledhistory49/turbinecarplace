# Turbine Car Place — Website UI Spec

**Color analysis based on the logo + flyer**

**Core palette extracted from both assets:**

| Role              | Color                  | Hex approximation     | Usage in brand assets |
|-------------------|------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| Primary           | Bright gold / amber-yellow | `#F5A623` – `#FFB800` | Wings, shield, “come to you”, accents, uniform stripes, icons |
| Secondary / Base  | Deep black             | `#0D0D0D` – `#111111` | Logo text, uniform, bottom CTA bar, car, typography |
| Neutral           | Pure white / off-white | `#FFFFFF` / `#F8F8F8` | Backgrounds, body text |
| Supporting        | Soft dark gray         | `#1A1A1A` – `#2A2A2A` | Subtle panels, text hierarchy |
| Accent highlight  | Slightly warmer gold   | `#E8A317`             | Hover states, stars, small details |

The logo is high-contrast black + gold on white. The flyer expands this into a premium dark-leaning composition: black uniforms and car against a light upper section, heavy use of the same gold for emphasis, and clean white space. The overall feel is **high-contrast, modern, and premium** rather than soft or pastel.

**Recommended website color application**
- Backgrounds: mostly clean white or very light gray for content sections, with strategic dark (near-black) hero / footer / CTA bands.
- Text: black for body, white on dark sections.
- Primary buttons & key highlights: the exact gold from the logo/shield.
- Secondary buttons / borders: black or dark gray with gold hover.
- Imagery should lean into glossy black cars, wet reflections, and the gold branding elements so the site feels cohesive with the flyer.

---

**Recommended website vibe**

**Go with a clean, modern premium service aesthetic** — not pure minimalist, not techy/sci-fi, and not heavy mechanical/industrial.

**Why this direction fits best:**
- The logo has a turbine/propeller motif, but the flyer completely softens it into a professional mobile detailing service. The photography (luxury black car, uniformed technician, wet reflections, clean residential backdrop) signals **premium care delivered to your door**, not a garage or industrial workshop.
- Messaging is “Premium Care. Delivered to You.” + “Trusted Experts” + diamond/shield icons. This is service-luxury, convenience, and trust — closer to high-end detailing brands than to a mechanical shop or a tech startup.
- Pure minimalist would feel too cold and sparse for a car-care business that wants to convey thoroughness and reliability.
- Full “techy” or cyber aesthetic would clash with the warm gold and the human/service-focused flyer.
- Heavy mechanical/steampunk or garage vibe would make the home-service offering feel less premium and less approachable.

**Concrete vibe description you should aim for:**
- Modern premium auto-detailing / mobile service brand.
- Clean layouts, generous but purposeful white space, strong typography hierarchy.
- High-quality photography of cars being detailed (especially black/luxury vehicles with water and shine).
- Subtle motion or hover effects on gold accents and buttons (nothing flashy).
- Professional yet approachable — the kind of site that feels trustworthy enough for someone to book a home service without hesitation.
- Structure example: bold hero with the main flyer-style message + strong “Book Home Service” CTA, clear service cards (Exterior, Interior, Full Detail, etc.), trust section with the three icons from the flyer, simple booking/contact form, and a dark footer with the two phone numbers.

This keeps the brand identity locked to the gold + black system while feeling elevated, contemporary, and service-oriented rather than mechanical or overly technical.
